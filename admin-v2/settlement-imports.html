Finance sends a sheet of payments drivers have already made to SheDrive. Each row is matched by national ID and applied as paid: less than she owes settles part of it, more than she owes adds the extra to her balance. Approved rows lift her go-online block once she is under the limit.

  1. Download template Get the template and fill it in
  2. Upload sheet Template and formats are checked
  3. Review result Business rules are checked
Step 1 / (Download template)
The payment details Finance fills in for each driver.

Fill in each column of the template as described below. Step 2 rejects a file that does not follow it.

national_id
Required. 14 digits. Matches the driver.
driver_name
Optional, up to 100 characters. Reference only — never used to match.
amount_egp
Required. A number above 0 with up to 2 decimals, no currency or commas (245.50). Less than she owes settles part of it; more adds to her balance.
payment_date
Required. The day she paid, YYYY-MM-DD, not in the future.
payment_channel
Required. Bank transfer, Fawry, Mobile wallet or Field agent, spelled exactly.
payment_reference
Required. Up to 60 letters, digits or hyphens. Unique — a reference already imported fails.
Step 2 / (Upload CSV file)
The file is checked against the template and every cell's format.
Select and upload the completed template (.csv).

Nothing is stored until a Financial lead approves the file.
