Finance sends a sheet of payments drivers have already made to SheDrive. Each row is matched by national ID and applied as paid: less than she owes settles part of it, more than she owes adds the extra to her balance. Approved rows lift her go-online block once she is under the limit.

Step 1 — Template
The columns Finance fills in
Column What it holds
national_idMatches the driver.
driver_nameReference only — never used to match.
amount_egpThe amount she paid — any amount. Less settles part of what she owes; more adds to her balance.
payment_dateThe day she paid (YYYY-MM-DD).
payment_channelBank transfer, Fawry, Mobile wallet or Field agent.
payment_referenceUnique. A reference already used is rejected.
